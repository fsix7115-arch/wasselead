What it does, in four steps

You paste in the leads you already have. It does the boring part — deciding who to reply to, and in what order.

1 · Ranks your leads

Someone asked a price question? Someone replied and got no answer? That jumps the queue. A plain "hello" waits.

2 · Tells you when

Hot leads are due in 2 hours, warm in a day, cold in two. You stop guessing and start working the list top-down.

3 · Writes the message

In your name and your business's voice. Put your prices in once and it answers the question people actually ask.

4 · Removes the wrong ones

Opt-outs, duplicate numbers written two different ways, and people who already booked never make it into your list.

What it deliberately doesn't do

These aren't missing features. Each one is a decision that protects your business number.

✕Doesn't send messages automatically

You paste each one into WhatsApp yourself. Bulk automation breaks WhatsApp's terms and gets your business number banned — that costs you far more than this tool does.

✕Doesn't message people who opted out

Unsubscribe requests are filtered out before anything reaches your list, and a test asserts it.

✕Doesn't message the same person twice

+91 98765 43210, 098765 43210 and 9876543210 are one person. They're treated as one, so nobody gets a duplicate.

✕Doesn't need a WhatsApp Business account

No paid API, no approval wait, nothing to configure. It works with the number you already use.

Questions people actually ask

Why doesn't it just send the messages for me?

Because auto-sending on WhatsApp gets your business number banned, and a banned number stops all your customer messages — not just this tool's. If the whole business depends on one number, that risk isn't worth ₹999. You keep control; we do the sorting.

Do I need to know coding?

No. It's one command with a CSV. I can set it up for you in the ₹2,999 plan, and if you can run a Python file you can run this yourself.

Will it work for Hindi or regional languages?

Not yet. It currently reads and writes English. It's on the list, and if you need it, say so and I'll tell you honestly whether it's ready or not.

What happens after I send a message?

You mark it done in your own sheet. The tool doesn't know whether they replied, and I'd rather it be honest about that than pretend to track it.

Is my customer data safe?

It runs on your machine. Leads never leave your computer, and there is no server, no account and no database on my side. The live demo on this page runs entirely in your browser.

What if it gives me bad advice about who to contact?

It doesn't give advice. It ranks by what people wrote and when. You're the one who decides — it just makes sure the person who asked a price question today isn't sitting below someone who said "hello" three days ago.
