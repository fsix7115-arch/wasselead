    06 — FAQ

    Questions

    Why won't it just send the messages itself?

    Because automatic bulk messaging is how WhatsApp numbers get banned. WasseLead never touches your account, so it can't get you in trouble. You stay in control, and the ban risk is zero.

    Do I need to know how to code?

    No. It's one command: python3 src/wasselead.py --leads yourleads.json --business yourbusiness.json. If you'd rather not run anything, the ₹2,999 setup includes me doing it with you on a call.

    What counts as a "hot" lead?

    Someone who asked a question, or mentioned price, charges, cost, rate, availability, timing or how much. Those get a two-hour reply window. Interested-but-not-sure leads get 24 hours, everything else gets 48.

    Will it message someone who said stop?

    No. Any lead marked as opted out is dropped before the queue is built, and shown separately so you know who was filtered and why.

    What if two people share a number?

    The second one is removed as a duplicate. Different formatting for the same number — 98765 43210, +91 98765 43210, 098765 43210 — is normalised first, so it still catches them.

    Where does my lead data go?

    Nowhere. The tool reads a file on your machine and writes output next to it. There is no server, no account, and no upload.
